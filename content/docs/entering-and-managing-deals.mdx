Every deal in SplitRE moves through a lifecycle. Understanding what each stage locks in place will save you from surprises later.

## Draft

Go to [Deals](https://app.splitre.app/deals) to start a new deal, or open an existing draft to keep editing it. While a deal is a draft:

- Click **Save Draft** to save your progress without confirming — nothing is emailed to the agent, and nothing is final.
- If you're editing an existing draft, the same button reads **Update Draft**.
- Drafts don't count toward cap totals yet.

## Confirm

When a deal is ready to be finalized, click **Confirm & Email Agent**. This:

- Locks the deal's numbers into a permanent breakdown snapshot (edits after this point require an amendment, not a direct edit).
- Recalculates the commission server-side — the numbers you see in the preview are double-checked, not just trusted from your browser.
- Assigns the deal a sequential **Deal #**, shown in its own column on the Deals list — a stable reference that doesn't repeat or change even if the address does (the same property can close more than once over the years).
- Updates the agent's cap ledger for the year.
- Emails the agent their commission breakdown.

An agent can never be confirmed into a negative payout — SplitRE blocks any confirmation that would leave the agent's net below $0.

## Splitting a deal between agents

A deal can be credited to more than one agent — a co-listing, a mentor and mentee, or an in-house deal where the listing side and the buyer side are both your agents. In the Close-a-Deal drawer, click **+ Add another agent** under the agent picker and give each agent a **GCI share %**. Up to six agents; the primary agent's share fills in automatically as whatever's left of 100%.

How the math works:

- **Whole-deal adjustments come off the top once.** An outside referral %, a relocation %, a bonus, or an ad-hoc deduction is applied to the deal as a whole before anything is split (an ad-hoc deduction is attributed to the primary agent).
- **Then each agent's share runs through their own terms.** Every agent's slice of what's left goes through their own commission plan — their split, their fees — and their own annual cap. One agent hitting their cap on the deal doesn't change what anyone else takes home.
- If two of the agents are on the same **team**, their slices draw the same shared team pool, walked in order so a single deal can't pull the pool past its cap. If an agent is on a team with a lead, they still pay the lead their split from their share of this deal — unless the lead is one of the co-agents, in which case the lead's share is simply their production.

It stays **one deal**: one GCI, one Deal #, one row on the Deals list (shown as e.g. "Jordan + 1"). Filtering the list by an agent includes every co-agent deal they're on, not just the ones where they're primary. The confirmation email — and any later amendment or void email — goes to you and to every agent on the deal, each showing the full breakdown.

**Amending** a co-agent deal changes deal-level fields only (price, commission %, closing date, the whole-deal adjustments). The set of agents and their shares are fixed once confirmed — to change who's on the deal or how it's split, void it and enter a new one.

## Amend

Need to fix a confirmed deal? Open it and click **Amend**, which takes you to an amendment form. Saving it (**Save Amendment**) creates a new deal record linked back to the original — the original stays in your history for an audit trail, and the amendment's numbers are what count going forward for cap and reporting purposes.

## Removing a deal

What "removing" means depends on whether the deal was ever confirmed:

- A **draft** was never a real transaction, so it can be deleted outright: open it and click **Delete draft**, then confirm. This permanently removes the record — it never counted toward cap totals, so nothing else is affected.
- A **confirmed** deal is a financial record — it's voided, never deleted, so there's always a trail of what actually happened. Open it and click **Void the deal**, then **Confirm void**. This reverses its contribution to the agent's cap ledger for the year and emails both you and the agent, since it's changing a record they may be relying on.
