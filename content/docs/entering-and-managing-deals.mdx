Every deal in SplitRE moves through a lifecycle. Understanding what each stage locks in place will save you from surprises later.

## Draft

Go to [Deals](https://app.splitre.app/deals) to start a new deal, or open an existing draft to keep editing it. While a deal is a draft:

- Click **Save Draft** to save your progress without confirming — nothing is emailed to the agent, and nothing is final.
- If you're editing an existing draft, the same button reads **Update Draft**.
- Drafts don't count toward cap totals yet.

## Confirm

When a deal is ready to be finalized, click **Confirm & Email Agent**. This:

- Locks the deal's numbers into a permanent breakdown snapshot (edits after this point require an amendment, not a direct edit).
- Recalculates the commission server-side — the numbers you see in the preview are double-checked, not just trusted from your browser.
- Updates the agent's cap ledger for the year.
- Emails the agent their commission breakdown.

An agent can never be confirmed into a negative payout — SplitRE blocks any confirmation that would leave the agent's net below $0.

## Amend

Need to fix a confirmed deal? Open it and click **Amend**, which takes you to an amendment form. Saving it (**Save Amendment**) creates a new deal record linked back to the original — the original stays in your history for an audit trail, and the amendment's numbers are what count going forward for cap and reporting purposes.

## Void

To reverse a deal entirely:

- On a **draft**, click **Void** — it's removed without affecting cap totals (it never counted toward them).
- On a **confirmed** deal, open it and click **Void the deal**, then **Confirm void**. This reverses its contribution to the agent's cap ledger for the year.
