Your agent roster lives at [Agents](https://app.splitre.app/agents). There are two ways to populate it.

## Add one agent at a time

1. On [Agents](https://app.splitre.app/agents), click **Add agent**.
2. Fill in **Full name** and **Email** (both required) and, optionally, assign a **Commission Plan** right away.
3. Click **Create agent**.

You'll land back on the Agents page with that agent's drawer already open, so you can immediately set their [cap opening balance](/docs/setting-up-agent-caps) or fine-tune plan overrides.

## Import your whole roster from a CSV

If you're migrating from a spreadsheet or another system, use [Import Agents](https://app.splitre.app/settings/import-agents) instead of adding agents one by one. It's a 4-step wizard:

1. **Upload** your CSV file.
2. **Map columns.** Match your spreadsheet's columns to SplitRE's fields:
   - Agent name (required)
   - Email (required)
   - Prior cap collected this year (optional; use this for agents joining mid-year, so their cap tracking starts accurate)
3. **Preview** the parsed rows before anything is created.
4. **Import.** Creates all the agents at once.

## Managing an existing agent

Click any agent's row on [Agents](https://app.splitre.app/agents) to open their drawer. Commission plan assignment, cap opening balance, and plan overrides all live there in one place.
