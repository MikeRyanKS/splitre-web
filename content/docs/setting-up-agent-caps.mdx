The cap is the maximum amount your brokerage collects from an agent in a calendar year — see [Creating a Commission Plan](/docs/creating-commission-plans) for how it factors into the calculation itself. This guide covers the one-time setup step: telling SplitRE how much of that cap an agent has already used.

## Cap opening balance

If an agent joins mid-year, or you're migrating from another system where they already had deals collected against their cap this year, you need to set their **cap opening balance** — otherwise SplitRE will think they're starting from $0 and they'll be under-capped for the rest of the year.

1. Go to [Agents](https://app.splitre.app/agents) and click the agent's row to open their drawer.
2. Find the **Cap Opening Balance** field — it's described there as the amount of broker cut already collected for this agent before they were added to SplitRE.
3. Enter the dollar amount and click **Save**. If they don't have a prior balance, use **Set to zero**.

## How the cap ledger works after that

Once the opening balance is set, SplitRE tracks the running total automatically — every confirmed deal for that agent adds a row to their cap ledger, with the cumulative total (opening balance + everything collected since) checked against the cap limit before each deal's broker cut is calculated. When the cumulative total reaches the cap, the agent gets 100% (or a flat post-cap fee, if the plan defines one) on every deal for the rest of that calendar year.

The cap year resets every January 1st, based on each deal's closing date — not the date it happens to be confirmed.

## Shared team caps

Some brokerages run a group of agents against **one combined cap** instead of a separate cap each. In SplitRE that's a **team**: any member's commission draws down the same pool, and every member flips to a 100% split at the same moment the pool reaches the team cap. A team can also have an optional lead who earns a cut of each member's team-deal commissions. Teams are set up from the Teams tab, not here — see [Teams and Shared Caps](/docs/teams) for the full walkthrough.

On a deal split between agents (see [Entering and Managing Deals](/docs/entering-and-managing-deals)), each agent's share still runs through their own cap — or their team's shared pool if they're on a team.
