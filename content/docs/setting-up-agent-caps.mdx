The cap is the maximum amount your brokerage collects from an agent in a cap year. See [Creating a Commission Plan](/docs/creating-commission-plans) for how it factors into the calculation itself. This guide covers the one-time setup step: telling SplitRE how much of that cap an agent has already used.

## Cap opening balance

If an agent joins mid-year, or you're migrating from another system where they already had deals collected against their cap this year, you need to set their **cap opening balance**. Otherwise SplitRE will think they're starting from $0 and they'll be under-capped for the rest of the year.

1. Go to [Agents](https://app.splitre.app/agents) and click the agent's row to open their drawer.
2. Find the **Cap Opening Balance** field. It's the amount this agent had already paid the brokerage toward their cap this cap year, before they were added to SplitRE. That is the brokerage's share of their commissions, not their total commission.
3. Enter the dollar amount and click **Save changes**. If they don't have a prior balance, use **Set to zero**.

The balance counts toward one cap year only: the one you enter it in. The agent's drawer says which. It doesn't carry into later years, so you never have to remember to clear it. Balances saved before October 2026 keep counting in every year until you save the balance again.

If the agent's plan has items with their own ceilings, a capped franchise royalty or companies sharing the broker cut, the drawer also asks how much was already paid toward each one this cap year. Those entries only count for the cap year you enter them in, so a royalty paid before you started with SplitRE in 2026 won't eat into 2027's allowance.

## How the cap ledger works after that

Once the opening balance is set, SplitRE tracks the running total automatically. Every confirmed deal for that agent adds a row to their cap ledger, with the cumulative total (opening balance plus everything collected since) checked against the cap limit before each deal's broker cut is calculated. When the cumulative total reaches the cap, the agent gets 100% (or a flat post-cap fee, if the plan defines one) on every deal for the rest of that cap year.

Each agent's drawer shows their cap progress, plus a separate bar for anything with its own ceiling, so you can see that the royalty is done for the year while the brokerage split still has room.

## See every deal that counted toward an agent's cap

Open [Reports > Agent Cap Report](https://app.splitre.app/reports/cap). You get one block per agent per cap year, and each row is a confirmed deal. It opens on the current cap year. Use the filters to pick one agent or switch to **All years**.

Each row shows:

- **GCI**: the commission on that deal.
- **Toward cap**: how much of the brokerage's share from that deal counted toward the agent's cap.
- **Total toward cap**: the running total for the cap year, after that deal.
- **Cap remaining**: how much more the agent pays before they cap.

The block header shows the agent's total against their cap, for example "$9,480.74 of $18,000.00 cap", and flags the deal where the cap was reached.

A few things you will see in the report:

- **$0 toward cap** means the agent was already capped on that deal, or the deal's type is set to sit outside the cap.
- **A "carried in" row** at the top is the agent's cap opening balance, with a link to where you set it.
- **A "Paid after cap" column** appears when a plan charges a flat fee after the cap. That fee is shown on its own and does not count toward the cap.
- **Team caps** show as their own block with a "Team cap" badge. The running total is the whole team's pool, so when a teammate's deals landed in between, a row says how much of the shared cap the other members used.
- **Deals split between agents** show each agent's own share.

Click **Download as spreadsheet (CSV)** to open the report in Excel or Google Sheets. Dates come out as MM/DD/YYYY, amounts are plain numbers, and each row has a description, so the file also imports into QuickBooks. The download follows whatever filters are on screen.

The agents on your [Dashboard](https://app.splitre.app/dashboard) show the same figures. The "See each deal toward cap" link there opens this report.

## Calendar year or anniversary year

By default every cap resets on January 1, based on each deal's closing date rather than the date you got around to confirming it.

Plenty of brokerages reset each agent's cap on the anniversary of the day they joined instead. To do that, go to [Commission Plans](https://app.splitre.app/plans) and set **Annual caps reset** to "on each agent's anniversary date". Then open each agent's drawer and enter their anniversary. A team's shared cap gets its own date on the team drawer.

Only the month and day matter, so the year you enter doesn't need to be the year they actually started. Agents you leave blank keep resetting on January 1. The drawer spells out the current window, for example "Sep 1, 2026 to Aug 31, 2027", so there's no guessing.

One thing to know about the annual earnings PDF (open an agent on [Agents](https://app.splitre.app/agents) and use **Earnings statement**): it covers a calendar year, so for an agent on an anniversary cap it reports what was collected from deals that closed in that year rather than a "so much of the cap used" figure, which would span two cap years and read as nonsense.

## Shared team caps

Some brokerages run a group of agents against **one combined cap** instead of a separate cap each. In SplitRE that's a **team**: any member's commission draws down the same pool, and every member flips to a 100% split at the same moment the pool reaches the team cap. A team can also have an optional lead who earns a cut of each member's team-deal commissions. Teams are set up from the Teams tab, not here. See [Teams and Shared Caps](/docs/teams) for the full walkthrough.

On a deal split between agents (see [Entering and Managing Deals](/docs/entering-and-managing-deals)), each agent's share still runs through their own cap, or their team's shared pool if they're on a team.
