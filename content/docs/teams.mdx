A **team** in SplitRE is a group of agents who share **one annual cap** instead of tracking a separate cap each. Commissions from any member draw down the same pool, and every member flips to a 100% split at the same moment: when the pool reaches the team's cap. A two-agent team on a $24,000 shared cap is done paying a split once your brokerage has collected $24,000 between them, no matter which agent generated it.

Teams are managed from the **Teams** tab on the [Agents](https://app.splitre.app/agents) page (also reachable from the **Teams** item in the sidebar). Nothing about teams is behind a pricing tier. Every plan includes them.

## Creating a team

1. Open the **Teams** tab and click **New team**.
2. Give it a **name** and a **shared annual cap** amount.
3. Optionally set an **opening balance**: company dollar already collected from these agents this year, before SplitRE. This works the same way a single agent's [cap opening balance](/docs/setting-up-agent-caps) does, just pooled.
4. Add **members** from your agent list.

Once a team exists, its deals show a **shared-cap progress bar** on the Dashboard, on the Agents page, and in the deal preview, in place of each member's individual bar. Revenue reports can be grouped by team.

## The optional team lead

A team lead is **optional**. Leave it blank and the team is a pure shared-cap pool: nothing changes about how each member's deal is calculated, and they just share one cap.

If you name a **lead** and a **percentage**, then on each member's team deal the lead earns that percentage of the member's commission:

- The lead's **own** deals never pay a split to themselves.
- Per deal, you can turn the lead's split **off** for a member's own sphere-of-influence deal that the lead had no part in. The deal still draws the shared cap. The team setting controls whether the split is on or off by default.
- Whether the lead's cut comes out of **the member's share** or **the full commission** is a setting on the team. The member takes home the same amount either way; the choice only changes whether your brokerage's own cut is reduced by the lead's portion.

## What happens to an agent's individual cap

While an agent is on a team, their individual cap and opening-balance settings are **set aside** and the team's shared cap applies instead. Their individual settings aren't deleted; they're just not in effect.

When an agent **leaves** a team, SplitRE asks where their individual cap tracking should resume:

- **From zero.** Start their individual cap fresh.
- **From what they personally contributed** to the team pool this year, carrying their own production forward.
- **A custom figure**, if you've agreed something else.

## Disbanding a team

Disbanding a team that has **closed deals** under it is non-destructive: every member goes back to tracking their own individual cap, but the closed deals and all their reporting stay exactly as they are. A team that never had a deal is simply removed.

## Teams and co-agent deals

If two agents on the **same team** are both on one [co-agent deal](/docs/entering-and-managing-deals), their shares draw the same shared team pool, walked in order so a single deal can't pull the pool past its cap. If a co-agent is on a team **with a lead**, they still pay the lead their split from their share of that deal, unless the lead is themselves one of the co-agents (in which case the lead's share is just their own production).
