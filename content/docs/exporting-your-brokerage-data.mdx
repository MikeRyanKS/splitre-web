For a complete copy of everything in your brokerage — separate from the per-deal QuickBooks export covered in [Sharing and Exporting Deals](/docs/sharing-and-exporting-deals) — go to [Data Export](https://app.splitre.app/settings/data-export) and click **Download my data**.

This downloads a single ZIP file containing:

- **deals.csv** — all confirmed deals with payouts
- **agents.csv** — your agent roster with plan assignments
- **cap_ledger.csv** — the full cap progress history for every agent

If you cancel your subscription, this export remains available for 30 days afterward — download a copy before that window closes if you're planning to leave.
