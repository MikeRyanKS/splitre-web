A fast reference for terms used throughout SplitRE and its documentation. For the full walkthroughs behind each concept, follow the linked guide.

### Agent Net

What an agent actually takes home from a deal: their share of the splittable amount, minus their own deductions (E&O, transaction fees, etc.). See [Creating a Commission Plan](/docs/creating-commission-plans).

### Amendment

A corrected version of a confirmed deal. Amending creates a new deal record linked back to the original, which stays in your history for an audit trail. See [Entering and Managing Deals](/docs/entering-and-managing-deals).

### Breakdown Snapshot

The frozen calculation record locked into a deal the moment it's confirmed. Later changes to a commission plan never retroactively change an already-confirmed deal's numbers. See [Entering and Managing Deals](/docs/entering-and-managing-deals).

### Broker Cut (Broker Net)

The portion of GCI the brokerage keeps, after commission plan rules and any cap limit are applied. See [Creating a Commission Plan](/docs/creating-commission-plans).

### Cap

The maximum dollar amount a brokerage collects from a single agent in a calendar year. Once hit, the agent keeps everything (or pays only a flat post-cap fee) for the rest of the year. See [Setting Up Agent Caps](/docs/setting-up-agent-caps).

### Cap Ledger

The running, deal-by-deal record of how much broker cut has been collected from each agent in a given year. This is what SplitRE checks before calculating whether an agent has hit their cap. See [Setting Up Agent Caps](/docs/setting-up-agent-caps).

### Cap Opening Balance

The amount of broker cut already collected from an agent before they were added to SplitRE, used so mid-year joiners' cap tracking starts accurate instead of at $0. See [Setting Up Agent Caps](/docs/setting-up-agent-caps).

### Cap Year

The 12-month window used for cap tracking, determined by a deal's closing date rather than the date it was confirmed. January 1 to December 31 by default, or starting on each agent's own anniversary date if the brokerage sets it up that way. See [Setting Up Agent Caps](/docs/setting-up-agent-caps).

### Closing Date

The date a deal closed, editable by the broker and defaulting to today. It determines which cap year a deal counts against. See [Entering and Managing Deals](/docs/entering-and-managing-deals).

### Co-agent Deal

One transaction credited to two or more agents (up to six), each with a GCI **share %**. Whole-deal deductions come off the top once; then each agent's share runs through their own commission plan and their own annual cap. It stays one deal with one Deal #. See [Entering and Managing Deals](/docs/entering-and-managing-deals).

### Commission Plan

The reusable template that defines how a deal is calculated for any agent assigned to it: the split, the cap, and any deductions. See [Creating a Commission Plan](/docs/creating-commission-plans).

### Confirmed

The deal status meaning a deal's numbers are locked into a breakdown snapshot and the agent has been emailed their commission breakdown. See [Entering and Managing Deals](/docs/entering-and-managing-deals).

### Counter Gate

A commission plan rule that applies an extra fee only to an agent's first N deals (for example, a mentorship fee on their first 3 closings). Counts deals in confirmation order, not closing order. See [Creating a Commission Plan](/docs/creating-commission-plans).

### Deal Override

A one-off customization (referral %, relocation %, bonus, custom deduction) applied to a single deal rather than the whole commission plan. Gets frozen into that deal's breakdown snapshot once confirmed. See [Creating a Commission Plan](/docs/creating-commission-plans).

### Deal Type

An optional category the broker picks on a deal, defined on the commission plan: a company-generated lead at a different split, or a rental for a flat fee to the brokerage. A type can also be marked as not counting toward the cap, so those deals don't move the agent's cap progress. Not available on a deal split between agents. See [Creating a Commission Plan](/docs/creating-commission-plans).

### Draft

The deal status before confirmation. Drafts can be freely edited and don't count toward an agent's cap yet. See [Entering and Managing Deals](/docs/entering-and-managing-deals).

### Fee Cap

A yearly ceiling on one specific fee, separate from the agent's main cap. A franchise royalty that stops once the agent has paid $3,000 for the year is a fee cap. On the deal that crosses the line, only the remaining amount is charged. See [Creating a Commission Plan](/docs/creating-commission-plans).

### Flat Deduction

A fixed dollar amount subtracted either off the top (before the split) or from the agent's share (after the split). An E&O insurance fee is one example. See [Creating a Commission Plan](/docs/creating-commission-plans).

### GCI (Gross Commission Income)

The starting number every calculation works from: sale price × commission percentage. See [Creating a Commission Plan](/docs/creating-commission-plans).

### Graduated Split (Tier)

A split that improves for the agent as they produce more, for example 70/30 up to $50,000 of year-to-date GCI and 80/20 after that. Tiers can be based on GCI, sales volume, broker cut paid, or closed deals, and they reset with the cap year. A deal that crosses a threshold is split across both tiers by default. See [Creating a Commission Plan](/docs/creating-commission-plans).

### Percentage Deduction

A percentage of GCI taken off the top before the agent/broker split is applied. A franchise royalty fee is one example. See [Creating a Commission Plan](/docs/creating-commission-plans).

### Plan Override

A per-agent adjustment to their assigned commission plan's split, cap, or deductions, without needing to create a separate plan just for them. See [Creating a Commission Plan](/docs/creating-commission-plans).

### Split

The agent/broker percentage division applied to the splittable amount, such as a 70/30 split. Must always total 100%. See [Creating a Commission Plan](/docs/creating-commission-plans).

### Split Company

One of two or more named companies that share the broker side of a deal, each with its own annual cap. Each takes a fixed amount per deal, a percentage of the broker share, or whatever is left, and each stops collecting on its own once its cap is reached. One of them is marked as your own brokerage; the rest are treated as money you collect and pass on, so they stay out of your revenue figures. See [Creating a Commission Plan](/docs/creating-commission-plans).

### Splittable Amount

What's left of the GCI after off-the-top deductions (franchise fee, referral %, relocation %, flat/percentage deductions) are subtracted. This is the number the split percentage is actually applied to. See [Creating a Commission Plan](/docs/creating-commission-plans).

### Team

A group of agents who share one annual cap: commissions from any member draw down the same pool, and every member flips to a 100% split at the same moment the pool reaches the team's cap. A team can also have an optional **team lead** who earns a set % of each member's commission on team deals. Teams are managed from the Teams tab, not the commission plan editor. See [Setting Up Agent Caps](/docs/setting-up-agent-caps).

### Team Split

The cut a team lead earns from each team member's commission on team deals: a set percentage, taken either from the member's post-split share or from the full commission (a team setting; the member's take-home is the same either way). Configured on the team, not as a commission plan rule. See [Setting Up Agent Caps](/docs/setting-up-agent-caps).

### Voided

The deal status for a deal that's been reversed. Voiding a confirmed deal reverses its contribution to the agent's cap ledger for the year. See [Entering and Managing Deals](/docs/entering-and-managing-deals).
