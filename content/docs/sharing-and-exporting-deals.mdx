Once a deal is confirmed, SplitRE gives you a few ways to get its numbers out to an agent or into your books — all from the same row of icons on the deal's row or drawer on [Deals](https://app.splitre.app/deals).

## Emailing an agent their breakdown

Confirming a deal (see [Entering and Managing Deals](/docs/entering-and-managing-deals)) automatically emails the agent their commission breakdown — you don't need to do anything extra for this. To resend it, click the email icon in the deal's action row.

## Downloading a PDF or copying a share link

Click the print icon to open a printable breakdown in a new tab (brokerage name, deal address, confirmation date, agent name, and the full commission numbers), then use **Download PDF** to save it via your browser's print dialog.

Click the share icon to copy a link to that same breakdown straight to your clipboard — useful when you'd rather paste it into an email or text than send it from SplitRE directly. The link doesn't require a login to view, so only share it with the agent it belongs to.

## Exporting confirmed deals for QuickBooks

To pull a batch of deals into your accounting software:

1. On [Deals](https://app.splitre.app/deals), select the confirmed deals you want (voided deals can't be selected).
2. Click **Export CSV for QuickBooks**.

This downloads a CSV formatted for QuickBooks import, with columns for date, address (split into street/city/state/zip), agent, sale price, commission %, GCI, agent net, broker net, and pre-written QBO invoice/bill descriptions. It only includes the deals you selected — for a full data dump of everything in your brokerage, see [Exporting Your Brokerage Data](/docs/exporting-your-brokerage-data) instead.
