Once a deal is confirmed, SplitRE gives you three ways to get its numbers out to an agent or into your books.

## Emailing an agent their breakdown

Confirming a deal (see [Entering and Managing Deals](/docs/entering-and-managing-deals)) automatically emails the agent their commission breakdown — you don't need to do anything extra for this. To resend it, open the deal and use the email icon in its action row.

## Sharing a deal as a link or PDF

Every confirmed deal has a shareable link. From the deal's row or drawer on [Deals](https://app.splitre.app/deals), click the print/PDF icon — this opens a public share page (no login required) in a new tab, showing:

- Brokerage name, deal address, and confirmation date
- Agent name
- The full commission breakdown: sale price, commission %, GCI, agent split %, agent gross, any referral/relocation/bonus adjustments, agent net, and broker cut

Anyone with the link can view it, so only share it with the agent it belongs to. From that page, click **Print / Save PDF** to save it as a PDF using your browser's print dialog.

## Exporting confirmed deals for QuickBooks

To pull a batch of deals into your accounting software:

1. On [Deals](https://app.splitre.app/deals), select the confirmed deals you want (voided deals can't be selected).
2. Click **Export CSV for QuickBooks**.

This downloads a CSV formatted for QuickBooks import, with columns for date, address (split into street/city/state/zip), agent, sale price, commission %, GCI, agent net, broker net, and pre-written QBO invoice/bill descriptions. It only includes the deals you selected — for a full data dump of everything in your brokerage, see [Exporting Your Brokerage Data](/docs/exporting-your-brokerage-data) instead.
