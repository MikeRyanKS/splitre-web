Once a deal is confirmed, SplitRE gives you a few ways to get its numbers out to an agent or into your books, all from the same row of icons on the deal's row or drawer on [Deals](https://app.splitre.app/deals).

## Emailing an agent their breakdown

Confirming a deal (see [Entering and Managing Deals](/docs/entering-and-managing-deals)) automatically emails the agent their commission breakdown. You don't need to do anything extra for this. To resend it, click the email icon in the deal's action row.

## Downloading a PDF or copying a share link

Click the print icon to open a printable breakdown in a new tab (brokerage name, deal address, confirmation date, agent name, and the full commission numbers), then use **Download PDF** to save it via your browser's print dialog.

Click the share icon to copy a link to that same breakdown straight to your clipboard. Anyone with the link can open it without a SplitRE login, so it's a good way to send an agent their numbers directly, or to paste into an email or text yourself. The link is a long random token, not a guessable deal number, so only someone you send it to can view that one deal.

## Exporting confirmed deals for QuickBooks

To pull a batch of deals into your accounting software:

1. On [Deals](https://app.splitre.app/deals), select the confirmed deals you want (voided deals can't be selected).
2. Click **Export CSV for QuickBooks**.

This downloads a CSV formatted for QuickBooks import, with columns for date, address (split into street/city/state/zip), agent, share %, sale price, commission %, GCI, agent net, broker net, and pre-written QBO invoice/bill descriptions. It only includes the deals you selected.

If any of the deals you picked passed money to another company, either a company sharing the broker cut or a fee your plan says is paid to someone specific, you also get a "Paid to" column for each of them. Those amounts are what your bookkeeper records as a bill. They are not included in broker net, which is your brokerage's own revenue. Deals with no such company produce exactly the same file as before.

There's a second export worth knowing about. Reports, then Broker Revenue, has a **By company** table showing what each company was paid across the deals in your date range, with its own Export CSV that lists every line item deal by deal. For a full data dump of everything in your brokerage, see [Exporting Your Brokerage Data](/docs/exporting-your-brokerage-data) instead.

A deal credited to more than one agent (see [Entering and Managing Deals](/docs/entering-and-managing-deals)) exports as **one row per agent**. Each row carries that agent's share %, their share of the deal's gross commission income, their agent net, and their broker net, so the rows still add up to the deal's totals, and each agent posts to your books as their own line.

For help setting up your QuickBooks Online chart of accounts to receive this export correctly (income accounts, agent vendor records, 1099 tracking), see [QuickBooks Online for Real Estate Brokerages: A Practical Setup Guide](/blog/quickbooks-online-real-estate-brokerage).
