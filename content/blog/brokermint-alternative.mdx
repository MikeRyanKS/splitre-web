Brokermint is now BoldTrail BackOffice, a broad system that pairs commission tracking with transactions, forms and payouts. SplitRE is the narrower alternative: it calculates commissions and tracks caps, and nothing else, for a published price. If you want one system for the whole brokerage, look hard at BoldTrail. If commission math is what keeps going wrong, SplitRE may be all you need.

![Two-column guide: choose one system for transactions, accounting and payouts, or choose commission math and cap tracking](/blog/brokermint-alternative/two-products-two-jobs-decision-guide.svg "The two products solve different jobs, so the right one depends on which job hurts.")

## How is BoldTrail BackOffice different from SplitRE?

We read BoldTrail's own BackOffice page in November 2026. It describes a back-office system and calls it the next generation of Brokermint. It lists commission automation, including splits, fees, sliding scales and unlimited commission plans. It also lists agent billing, next-day ACH disbursements, a chart of accounts, transaction checklists, forms, eSignature and an integration with QuickBooks. It publishes no prices and asks you to request a demo.

SplitRE does less, on purpose. It calculates each deal from your commission plans, tracks every agent's cap, and records the result. It handles teams, shared caps, co-agent deals and caps split across two companies. It exports a QuickBooks-ready CSV. It does not run transactions, collect signatures, bill agents or send payouts, and it has no live QuickBooks connection. You should know that before you compare features.

## Which Brokermint alternative fits which brokerage?

Pick the one that matches the job you need done.

- **You want one place for transactions, accounting and agent payouts.** A broad system such as BoldTrail BackOffice is closer to that. Ask for a demo and for a written quote.
- **You already have transaction management and a bookkeeper, and commission math is the problem.** A focused tool is simpler to adopt, and every feature is on one flat tier. SplitRE's [pricing](/pricing) is public, and your first 3 deals are free.
- **You run several commission structures.** Check that each tool handles them natively, whether that is caps that reset on an anniversary, fees that stop at a yearly ceiling, or teams.

Whichever way you lean, put three questions to every vendor in writing. What does it cost when I add an agent or close more deals? Which of my commission rules are built in, and which need a workaround? What happens to my data if I leave? A vendor who answers plainly is worth talking to.

Be wary of any comparison that declares one product better for everyone. Our [earlier comparison of commission software](/blog/best-commission-split-software-for-brokerages) makes the same point.

## How do you test commission software before you commit?

Run the same awkward deal through each tool.

Example: a $500,000 sale at 3% is $15,000 in gross commission. The agent is on a 70/30 split, so the brokerage would normally take $4,500. But this agent has only $2,000 of room left under an $18,000 cap. The brokerage should collect $2,000, not $4,500, and the agent should keep $13,000 before fees.

Now try the harder cases: the same deal with an outside referral, a deal split between two agents, and a deal backdated to last December. If a tool cannot reproduce your own numbers, it will not match your agents' expectations either.
