The three commission tools independent brokerages compare most often price themselves in three genuinely different ways: **Paperless Pipeline** charges by transaction volume, **Brokermint** (rebranded **BoldTrail BackOffice** in 2024, after its parent company Inside Real Estate folded it into the same family as kvCORE) quotes on request, and **SplitRE** publishes a flat rate by agent-count tier, or a pay-per-deal option, with no per-transaction fee on the tiers. None of these models is wrong. They're bets on how your brokerage will grow, and the right one depends on whether your headcount or your deal volume is the number you expect to climb.

This isn't a "why we're better" post. It's the comparison we'd want if we were shopping for this software ourselves: how each one prices as you scale, and how each one handles commission caps and tiered splits specifically, since that's the feature independent brokerages tend to care about most.

## How each one prices access

**Paperless Pipeline** bills by monthly closed-transaction volume, not by agent count. Its own pricing page lists unlimited users on every plan, with the cost climbing in steps as your transaction count does. Check the current figures on their site, because they change. Its commission-tracking features (splits, caps, agent statements) are a separate paid module on top of the base transaction-management plan, not included by default.

**Brokermint / BoldTrail BackOffice** doesn't publish pricing publicly. You request a demo and get a quote. Ask how that quote changes as you add agents and as your deal volume grows, and get the answer in writing.

**SplitRE** publishes a flat rate per tier based on how many active agents you have, billed monthly or annually, with every feature included at every tier. There's no per-transaction meter on the tiers: once you're in one, closing more deals doesn't raise the bill. If you close too few deals for a monthly plan, there's also a pay-as-you-go option where you buy deal credits and spend one per confirmed deal. Your first 3 deals are free either way. Current figures are on the [pricing page](/pricing).

## What that means as you grow

If your brokerage adds agents but each one closes only a handful of deals a year, transaction-based pricing (Paperless Pipeline) can end up cheaper than per-agent pricing. If your agents are high-volume and few in number, the reverse can be true. If you don't want to model either scenario and would rather know your software cost in January regardless of how the year goes, flat agent-tier pricing removes the variable entirely, which is the trade-off SplitRE is built around.

## Cap tracking and tiered splits

This is the feature independent brokerages searching for any of these three tools tend to actually need, since it's also the easiest thing to get wrong in a spreadsheet. See [The Real Estate Commission Cap, Explained](/blog/real-estate-commission-cap-explained) for why.

- **Paperless Pipeline**: cap tracking, tiered splits, and agent statements live in its optional Commission module, priced separately from the base transaction-management plan.
- **Brokermint / BoldTrail BackOffice**: commission automation (described on their own site as handling "splits, fees, sliding scales, and unlimited commission plans") is part of the core back-office product.
- **SplitRE**: cap tracking, graduated splits, anniversary-year caps, and per-agent plan overrides are included in every tier. Cap balances update automatically the moment a deal is confirmed, and a deal that crosses an agent's cap mid-transaction is split correctly at the crossing point without any manual adjustment.

## Which one fits which brokerage

A brokerage with a handful of very high-producing agents and infrequent hiring might do fine on transaction-based pricing. A brokerage that expects to add agents steadily and wants commission tracking bundled in from day one, without requiring a sales call to find out what it costs, is the brokerage SplitRE is built for.

## FAQ

**Does SplitRE charge per agent or per transaction?**
Neither. SplitRE's three tiers are priced by how many active agents your brokerage has (up to 10, up to 30, or unlimited), and every tier includes unlimited deals and every feature, with no separate module to buy and no per-closing fee. A pay-as-you-go option charges per confirmed deal instead, and your first 3 deals are free.

**Is Brokermint still called Brokermint?**
The product is still commonly searched under its original name, but Inside Real Estate rebranded it to BoldTrail BackOffice in 2024 as part of consolidating Brokermint, kvCORE, and BoomTown under the BoldTrail brand.

**Do I need the Commission module if I use Paperless Pipeline?**
Cap tracking, tiered splits, and agent statements are part of Paperless Pipeline's separate Commission add-on, not the base transaction-management plan. Confirm current pricing on their site, since it's billed on top of your transaction-volume tier.
